# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

*Draft for execution · 22 November 2026 · Not legal advice: have a qualified lawyer review it before anyone signs*

**Agreement reference:** NDA-SMS-2026-001 · **Project:** CampusCore (project code SMS-2026-001)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on **\[date\]** (the “Effective Date”) between:

1. **Acme Institute of Technology**, of 123 Example Street, Sampletown, 000000 (“Party A”); and
2. **Globex Software Ltd**, of 456 Sample Avenue, Exampleville, 000000 (“Party B”).

Each is a “Party” and together they are the “Parties”. Because information flows both ways, each Party is a “Discloser” when it discloses Confidential Information and a “Recipient” when it receives it.

## Background

A. The Parties wish to discuss, plan, design, build and test a Student Management System called CampusCore, project code SMS-2026-001 (the “Project”).

B. In doing so, each Party expects to disclose confidential information to the other.

C. The Parties have agreed to protect that information on the terms below.

## 1. Definitions

- **Affiliate** means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party.
- **Authorised Tool** means a third-party software tool or AI service approved under clause 6.
- **Personal Data** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, including students, parents, guardians and staff.
- **Purpose** means evaluating, negotiating, planning, designing, developing, testing, deploying and supporting the Project, and nothing else.
- **Representatives** means a Party’s and its Affiliates’ directors, officers, employees and contractors, and its professional advisers (such as lawyers, accountants and auditors), who need to know Confidential Information for the Purpose.
- **Security Incident** means any accidental or unauthorised access to, use, disclosure, loss, alteration or destruction of Confidential Information.

## 2. Confidential Information

**2.1** “Confidential Information” means all non-public information, in any form (written, oral, electronic or visual, or learned through access to systems, repositories or environments), that a Discloser, its Affiliates or its Representatives disclose to the Recipient in connection with the Purpose, before or after the Effective Date, and that is marked or described as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential from its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.

**2.2** It includes, without limitation:

- product requirements, specifications, roadmaps, designs, architectures, database schemas and API definitions;
- source code, object code, algorithms, configuration, test data and test results;
- security information, including vulnerabilities, audit findings, credentials, keys, and network and system details;
- business, financial, pricing and commercial information;
- information about the Discloser’s institution, staff, students and parents;
- the existence and terms of this Agreement and the discussions about the Project; and
- notes, analyses and summaries prepared by the Recipient that contain or reflect any of the above.

## 3. Exclusions

**3.1** Information is not Confidential Information to the extent the Recipient can show from written records that it:

- is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement;
- was lawfully known to the Recipient, free of any duty of confidence, before the Discloser disclosed it;
- was lawfully received from a third party that was free to disclose it; or
- was independently developed by the Recipient without using or referring to the Confidential Information.

**3.2** Information is not public merely because parts of it are public, or because it is covered by more general public information. These exclusions do not apply to Personal Data or credentials.

## 4. The Recipient’s obligations

The Recipient must:

1. use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
2. keep it confidential and disclose it only to its Representatives who need to know it for the Purpose;
3. protect it with at least the care it gives its own most sensitive information, and never less than reasonable care, including access controls and, where reasonably practicable, encryption in transit and at rest;
4. make sure each Representative is bound by written or professional duties of confidentiality at least as protective as this Agreement, and remain responsible for their acts and omissions as if they were its own;
5. copy it only as reasonably needed for the Purpose; and
6. not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any software, prototype or other material provided by the Discloser.

## 5. Security incidents

The Recipient must tell the Discloser in writing, without undue delay and within 48 hours at the latest, after becoming aware of a Security Incident. The notice must describe what happened and what information is affected. The Recipient must take reasonable steps to contain and remedy the incident and must cooperate with the Discloser’s investigation.

## 6. Software tools and AI services

**6.1** The Recipient may enter Confidential Information into a third-party software tool or AI service only if it is listed in Schedule 2, or the Discloser has approved it in writing (email is enough) before first use.

**6.2** Each Authorised Tool must meet all of these conditions:

- the provider does not use the information to train or improve models or services for anyone else;
- the provider keeps it only as long as needed to deliver the service, and it can be deleted on request;
- access is limited to the Recipient’s Representatives; and
- the provider’s terms protect confidentiality at least as well as this Agreement.

**6.3** Credentials, keys, secrets and Personal Data must never be entered into any third-party tool or AI service, authorised or not.

**6.4** Output that a tool generates from Confidential Information is itself Confidential Information of the Discloser to the extent it contains or reflects that information. The Recipient will keep Schedule 2 up to date and give the Discloser a current list within 5 business days of a request.

## 7. Personal Data

**7.1** The Parties do not intend to exchange real Personal Data under this Agreement. Development, testing and demonstrations will use synthetic or anonymised data.

**7.2** If real Personal Data must be shared, the Parties will first sign a data protection agreement, or adopt terms, that comply with the data protection law that applies, and will share only the minimum needed.

**7.3** A Party that receives Personal Data in breach of this clause must promptly tell the other Party, restrict access to it, and securely delete it when instructed.

## 8. Disclosure required by law

**8.1** If the law, a court or a regulator requires the Recipient to disclose Confidential Information, the Recipient must, where lawful, tell the Discloser promptly in writing so that it can seek a protective order, disclose only the part that is legally required, and use reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment.

**8.2** Information disclosed under this clause remains Confidential Information for all other purposes.

## 9. Ownership and no licence

**9.1** Confidential Information, and all intellectual property rights in it, remain the property of the Discloser or its licensors.

**9.2** No licence or other right is granted, expressly or by implication, except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose.

**9.3** This Agreement does not decide who owns software, designs or other work produced in the Project, or the commercial terms of any engagement. Those must be set out in a separate written agreement: **\[reference, or “to be agreed”\]**.

## 10. Return and deletion

**10.1** Within 14 days after the Discloser’s written request, or when the Purpose ends or this Agreement is terminated, the Recipient must return or securely and permanently delete all Confidential Information in its or its Representatives’ possession or control, and confirm in writing, signed by an authorised officer, that it has done so.

**10.2** The Recipient may keep copies that the law or its bona fide compliance policies require it to keep, and copies in routine electronic backups that are not readily accessible, until they are deleted or overwritten in the ordinary course. Those copies remain subject to this Agreement for as long as they are kept, even after it expires.

## 11. Term and survival

**11.1** This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and runs for 2 years (the “Disclosure Period”), unless either Party ends it earlier by giving 30 days’ written notice.

**11.2** Each Recipient’s duties continue for 3 years after the Disclosure Period ends, except that they continue (a) for source code and trade secrets, for as long as they remain trade secrets or confidential under the law, and (b) for Personal Data, for as long as the Recipient holds it.

**11.3** Termination or expiry does not affect rights that have already accrued, and clauses 4 to 10 and 12 to 16 continue to apply as far as needed to give effect to them.

## 12. No warranty and no commitment

Confidential Information is provided as is. The Discloser gives no warranty that it is accurate, complete or free of third-party rights. Neither Party must disclose any particular information or enter into any further agreement. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, agency, joint venture or employment relationship.

## 13. Remedies

**13.1** A breach of this Agreement may cause harm that damages alone cannot repair. The Discloser may seek an injunction, specific performance or other equitable relief, without proving actual loss and, where the law allows, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**13.2** Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability that cannot lawfully be limited or excluded.

## 14. Notices

Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent by hand, by courier or by email to the contact in Schedule 1. A notice is received on delivery by hand or courier. A notice sent by email is received when sent if that is before 5 pm on a business day at the recipient’s location, and otherwise on the next business day, unless the sender receives a delivery failure message. A Party may change its contact by notice to the other.

## 15. Governing law and disputes

**15.1** This Agreement is governed by the laws of **\[jurisdiction\]**, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

**15.2** The Parties will first try to settle any dispute through good-faith discussions between senior representatives for 30 days. This does not stop either Party from seeking urgent relief under clause 13.

**15.3** If the dispute is not settled, the courts of **\[place\]** have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 16. General

- **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the whole agreement between the Parties about its subject and replaces earlier understandings on it. It applies alongside any data protection or services agreement between the Parties. If they conflict on confidentiality, the more protective term applies.
- **Changes.** Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
- **Waiver.** A delay or failure to exercise a right is not a waiver of it.
- **Severability.** If a provision is unenforceable, the rest remains in force.
- **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the other’s prior written consent.
- **Publicity.** Neither Party will use the other’s name or logo, or announce the Project, without prior written consent.
- **Third parties.** Only the Parties may enforce this Agreement.
- **Costs.** Each Party bears its own costs.
- **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts and electronically, and each counterpart is an original.
